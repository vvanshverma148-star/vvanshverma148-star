## About Me

I'm a Computer Science undergrad at **Jaypee Institute of Information Technology** (B.Tech CSE, 2024–2028, CGPA 8.47) who likes building things end-to-end — from matching product data across e-commerce sites, to grounding LLM answers in real medical documents, to mapping urban heat islands from satellite data.

- **AI Intern @ IBM SkillsBuild** — built and shipped **MediEvidence AI**, a medical RAG chatbot, as the capstone of a 6-week AI internship
- Completed **Deloitte Australia job simulations** in both **Data Analytics** (dashboards, data-driven insights) and **Cybersecurity** (threat analysis, risk mitigation)
- Competing in **Samsung Innovate-X**, **Bhartiya Antriksha Hackathon**, and **Tata Innovent 2026** — national-level AI/deep-tech competitions
- Scored **98.75% in Class XII (ISC)** and **97.40% in Class X (ICSE)**
- Off the keyboard: primary vocalist, video editor (Premiere Pro), and I post song covers on Instagram
